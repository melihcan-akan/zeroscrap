Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Zero Scrap does not collect any personal data.

  • The game does not ask you to create an account.
  • Your progress, daily results and settings are stored only on your own phone. Deleting the game deletes them.

No ads. The game contains no ads, tracking or analytics tools. It does not connect to the internet.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
